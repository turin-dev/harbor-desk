# Code of conduct

Harbor Desk is committed to a respectful, constructive, and harassment-free
community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and lived experiences.
- Give and receive feedback about code and behavior in good faith.
- Focus on the technical issue, not personal attacks.
- Respect privacy. Do not disclose credentials, private host details, personal
  information, or non-public security reports.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, deliberate disclosure of
private information, persistent disruption, and other conduct that makes
participation unsafe or unwelcome are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns through the private maintainer channel configured in
the public repository. Maintainers may remove content, issue a warning, limit
participation, or take other proportionate action. Retaliation against a person
who reports a concern is not acceptable.
